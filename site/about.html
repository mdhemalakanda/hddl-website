Who We Are

Design and development under one roof

Hyperion Design & Development Ltd. is a real estate company specializing in superior quality residential buildings in the prime locations of Dhaka city. Per its published profile, the company started its journey in 2000 “to make quality and excellence in the apartment sector”, has completed one hundred and two (102) apartment buildings that were handed over to clients, and has twenty-one (21) apartment building projects at hand.

The company describes itself as a trusted name in the real estate sector in Bangladesh, and is an active member of the Real Estate & Housing Association of Bangladesh (REHAB).

Source note: company facts and figures on this page are as published on the developer’s bdhousing.com profile (retrieved November 2026). The profile states both “started its journey in 2000” and “during the last 13 years”; we present the company’s statements as published.
What We Build

Security & Safety

Security, CCTV, security alarm systems, electronic security, fire exits and fire protection are listed among the amenities of our buildings.

Utilities & Backup

Generator, electricity, WASA connection, self water supply, Titas Gas and hot water are listed across our buildings.

Connected Living

Lifts, intercom, telephone lines, Wi-Fi connectivity and satellite or cable TV points are listed building amenities.

Added Comforts

Solar panels, gardens and prayer spaces appear in our listings — and the Park View buildings in Banasree also list a pool and a gymnasium.
